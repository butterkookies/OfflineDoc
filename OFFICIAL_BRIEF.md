FAQ · 1 OF 7
Rules and tools
Can we use cloud APIs?
Yes, as secondary components. Your core Local AI functionality 
must not depend entirely on a cloud AI API.
Can we use AI coding tools like Devin?
Yes. AI-assisted development and Devin are allowed. Disclose the 
tools you used.
Can we use open-source models and libraries?
Yes. Disclose every model, framework, and API in your submission.
Can we reuse code we wrote before today?
Your project must be substantially built during the hackathon. 
Disclose any existing code or assets.
Do we need a speciﬁc model, OS, or hardware?
No. Any model, framework, operating system, or hardware platform 
works.
Can people outside the hackathon help us?
No. Participants proven to have received external help from people 
outside the hackathon are disqualified.

Pitches and awards
How long is each pitch?
5 minutes to pitch and demo live, plus 3 minutes of judge Q&A.
How does People's Choice voting work?
People at Demo Day scan a QR code to vote. The same QR code 
scans are also used for the raffle.
Can one project win more than one award?
Yes. Special awards can be won independently of the main award.
How are cash prizes paid?
By bank transfer to an authorized representative from each winning 
team.
Do we keep ownership of what we build?
Yes. Your team keeps ownership of your project.
Can results be disputed?
Yes, if a team is proven to have violated the rules, including a 
pre-existing project, external help, or fake benchmarks.
